## Breaking

- **Style-locks** — MD004 dash, MD035 `---`, MD046 fenced, MD048 backtick, MD050
  asterisk. Consumers that drifted from these styles will fail until fixed.
- **MD044** — conservative proper-names list (`GitHub`, `JavaScript`,
  `TypeScript`, `Node.js`, `markdownlint`, `Linear`); shorter format/tool names
  that false-positive on filenames were omitted.
- **MD049** — italics must use underscores (`_like this_`). Run
  `markdownlint-cli2 --fix` for most conversions. Estate `AGENTS.md` needs the
  same change in `shared-agents-md` before the next fan-out ([R-1200](https://linear.app/rheged-studio/issue/R-1200)).

## Changed

- Hardened `.markdownlint.jsonc` with the locks and rules above; bumped the
  audit date; documented that MD041 stays disabled pending [R-1199](https://linear.app/rheged-studio/issue/R-1199) (pamphleteer
  role frontmatter and similar consumer hits).
- Converted this repo's `AGENTS.md` italics to underscores and fixed
  `Markdownlint` → `markdownlint` link labels in `README.md`.
